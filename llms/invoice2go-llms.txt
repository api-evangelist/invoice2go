# Invoice2go

> Invoice2go (part of BILL) is a mobile-first invoicing and small-business management app for freelancers, contractors, and self-employed professionals. It lets users create and send professional invoices and estimates, accept online payments (bank transfer, credit/debit card, PayPal), track expenses and time, and view business reports across web, iOS, and Android. Invoice2go has ~225,000 small-business users. There is no live public developer API; third-party integrations are delivered through the Apideck-powered integrations marketplace.

## Product
- [Invoice2go](https://invoice2go.com): Invoicing, estimates, payments, and business management for small businesses.
- [Send invoices](https://invoice2go.com/send-invoices/): Create and send professional invoices.
- [Accept payments](https://invoice2go.com/accept-payments/): Online card, bank transfer, and PayPal payments.
- [Estimating software](https://invoice2go.com/estimating-software/): Professional estimates to win work.
- [Business reports](https://invoice2go.com/business-reports/): Analytics on cash flow and business health.
- [Mobile app](https://invoice2go.com/mobile-app/): iOS and Android apps.
- [Pricing](https://invoice2go.com/pricing/): Subscription plans.
